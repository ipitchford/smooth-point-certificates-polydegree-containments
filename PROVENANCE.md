1. The original v0.2.0 archive combined a divisor-theory note and a finite
   Polydegree certificate extension.
2. The source was subsequently reorganised as
   `polydegree-hensel-certificates-e3-v0.3.0`; its SHA-256 is
   `e51af558a067c28847219a788f8dd4d28de4a6e9bc3b8d00c5ecab2c3b123334`.
3. The v0.3.0 witness JSON has SHA-256
   `b659a8bccea30df4f800df526319bcbe01f429c3bee2ee8146983933da9cb38b`
   and is byte-identical to the v0.2.0 witness corpus audited in Stage 1.
   Stage 2.5 now carries those earlier bytes at
   `source_records/stage1_v0.2/polydegree_e3_witnesses_50_100.json`, so the
   comparison is directly replayable inside this package.
4. Stage 1 extracted the general derivative, determinant and weighted-Euler
   identities, sharpened the Hensel theorem, repaired the curve bridge,
   completed exact geometry sweeps and conducted a bounded prior-art audit.
5. Stage 2 reconciled the newer upstream archive without overwriting either
   frozen source, drafted the present theorem paper and Evidence Press layer,
   and initiated three independent uniform-proof attacks.
6. One attack produced an all-index boundary-squarefreeness proof. An
   independent referee found that its first strict-interlacing citation was
   too strong; the proof was repaired using logarithmic-mesh preservation and
   only the repaired version was integrated.
7. Stage 2.5 corrected citation metadata and context, removed unsupported
   author declarations, aligned machine-readable AI provenance and claim
   polarity, and replayed the mathematical evidence without changing the
   theorem boundary.
8. Automated tools assisted proof exploration, exact computation, adversarial
   review, drafting and packaging under human direction. They are not authors.

The source archives and Stage 1 package remain immutable evidence layers. This
candidate is an additive derivative work, not a silent rewrite of either.
