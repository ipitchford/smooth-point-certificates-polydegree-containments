# Bilingual abstracts

## English

Lewis, Perry and Straub introduced integral coefficient polynomials and an
auxiliary determinant whose complex specialisations imply Polydegree
containments for plane polynomial automorphisms. This paper identifies the
determinant, up to sign, as the Jacobian of the coefficient map. Weighted
Euler identities then give an exact syzygy that converts the original
non-vanishing condition into a smooth-point condition: the partial coefficient
complete intersection must have one smooth affine point outside the next
coefficient hypersurface. The reformulation yields a smaller finite-field
certificate. A simple modular common zero with non-zero next coefficient
lifts to characteristic zero, even when the prime divides the scalar that
makes the original determinant vanish modulo that prime. A complementary
curve theorem shows how a reduced affine zero divisor supplies the same
Jacobian point. Exact computations for the first non-trivial column, (e=3),
prove that the whole affine intersection is reduced and avoids the next
coefficient for (2\le d\le20). The one-variable boundary factors are
uniformly squarefree, while adjacent coprimality is checked through
(d=200). The finite affine range and bounded adjacent test motivate a
uniform conjecture but are not presented as an all-(d) affine theorem. Written proofs, inherited
results, exact finite evidence and open questions are recorded as distinct
evidence classes.

**Keywords:** plane polynomial automorphisms; Polydegree Conjecture; Jacobian
criterion; multivariate Hensel lifting; complete intersections; exact
computation

## Traditional Chinese

Lewis、Perry 與 Straub 以整係數多項式及一個輔助行列式，建立平面多項式自同構之多重次數層閉包包含判準。本文指出該行列式在符號差異下正是係數映射的 Jacobian 行列式，並利用加權 Euler 恆等式導出整係數 syzygy，將原本的非消失條件改寫為幾何條件：部分係數完全交必須有一個光滑仿射點，且該點不落在下一個係數超曲面上。此改寫縮小了有限體證書；只要模質數的共同零點為單根，且下一係數非零，即可藉多變量 Hensel 提升至特徵零，即使該質數整除使原行列式在有限體中消失的純量亦然。本文另證明一個曲線上的約化零因子可產生相同光滑點的橋接定理。對 (e=3) 的精確計算顯示，(2\le d\le20) 時整個仿射交皆約化且避開下一係數；本文亦證明每一個一變量邊界因子皆只有單純正實根，故一致平方自由，而相鄰邊界因子互質則精確檢查至 (d=200)。有限仿射範圍與有界相鄰檢查支持但不證明全 (d) 仿射猜想。全文嚴格區分已證定理、外部繼承結果、有限精確證據與未解問題。

**關鍵詞：** 平面多項式自同構；多重次數猜想；Jacobian 判準；多變量 Hensel 提升；完全交；精確計算
