# Supplementary proof-search status

Three structural attacks were run after the core Jacobian manuscript was
drafted. Their outputs are retained because they sharpen the research
frontier, but only the independently repaired squarefreeness theorem was
integrated into the manuscript.

## Uniform boundary squarefreeness

**Status: proved and integrated as Theorem 7.2.** The reciprocal boundary
factor is a terminating ${}_3F_2$ polynomial and factors as a degree-matched
multiplicative finite free convolution of two Jacobi ${}_2F_1$ factors. An
independent referee rejected the first strict-interlacing shortcut and
replaced it with the applicable logarithmic-mesh theorem. The corrected proof,
exact replay and primary-source PDFs are retained in `evidence/geometry/` and
`source_records/proof_referee/`.

This theorem proves that each individual boundary factor is squarefree. It
does not prove adjacent coprimality.

## Triple disjointness

**Status: open, with proved reductions.** The all-index raising identity

$$
V(G_n)=(n+2)G_{n+1}-(3nX-4n-2)G_n
$$

shows that a triple zero of (G_d,G_{d+1},G_{d+2}) must be a non-transverse
intersection of the first two curves. The full (Y=0) slice is also excluded.
The residual non-transverse case remains open. Exact replay covers the stated
bounded range and is not an all-index proof.

## Toric affine nondegeneracy

**Status: open, with a uniform face ledger.** Newton support, facet
classification, the mixed-volume formula and the (Y=0) face coprimality
theorem are proved uniformly. Two other face families and the independent
interior Jacobian and next-coefficient unit-ideal conditions remain open.
Exact full-affine evidence reaches (d=30), and exact face evidence reaches
(d=250); these extensions do not replace the manuscript's conservative
(d=20) and (d=200) evidence boundaries.

## Claim rule

No finite extension, source hash, replay agreement or internal referee report
is represented as an all-index affine theorem, independent reproduction,
external specialist acceptance or peer review.
